# Introduction

Welcome to the **RavenSS detections guide.**

Here you will find **detailed descriptions** of **modules**, their **detectability**, and optimized **configurations** for both free and premium ghost clients. Our goal is to keep you updated on what is currently safe to use and what will get you banned instantly.

---

### What You Will Find Here

* **[Configs](./Configs)** - Prepared, optimized settings for mainstream clients like **Slinky** and **Vape** which have been tested and made by our staff.
* **Module Breakdown** - Explaining how modules work and the ideal settings for bypassing anti-cheats.
* **Detection Statuses** - Up-to-date information on which modules are easily detected by modern anti-cheats (AGC, Polar, Watchdog etc.) or during screenshares.

---

### Our Safety Rating System

To make the guide easy to use, we categorize settings and modules using three simple levels:

* 🟢 **Safe / Closet** - Highly bypassable. These settings are mathematically designed to look completely legitimate, both to anti-cheats and staff, with the assumption that you have reasonable settings.
* 🟡 **Risky / Experimental** - Use with caution. These settings might bypass some anti-cheats but are highly susceptible to staff bans.
* 🔴 **Blatant / Detected** - Instant ban territory. These modules or values are either heavily detected by modern anti-cheats or will get you staff banned in seconds.
