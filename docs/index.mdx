# Introduction

Welcome to the **RavenSS detections guide.**

Here you will find **detailed descriptions** of **modules**, their **detectability**, and optimized **configurations** for both free and premium ghost clients. Our goal is to keep you updated on what is currently safe to use and what will get you banned instantly.

---

### What You Will Find Here

* **Configs** - Prepared, optimized settings for mainstream clients like **Slinky**, **Vape** and **Drip Lite** which have been tested and made by our staff.
* **Module Breakdown** - Explaining how modules work and the ideal settings for bypassing anticheats.
* **Detection Statuses** - Up to date information on which modules are detected by modern anticheats (AGC, Polar, Watchdog etc.)

---

### Our Safety Rating System

To make the guide easy to use, we categorize settings and modules using three simple levels:

* <img src="/docs/img/safe.svg" style={{width: '24px', height: '24px', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} /> **Safe** - These modules are designed to look completely legitimate, both to anticheats and staff, with the assumption that you're using reasonable settings.
* <img src="/docs/img/risky.svg" style={{width: '24px', height: '24px', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} /> **Risky** - These settings might bypass anticheats but are highly susceptible to staff and anticheat bans if configured incorrectly.
* <img src="/docs/img/blatant.svg" style={{width: '24px', height: '24px', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} /> **Blatant** - These modules or values are either heavily detected by anticheats or will get you staff banned in seconds.
