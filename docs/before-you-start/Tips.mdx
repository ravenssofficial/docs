# Useful Tips

  A collection of essential practices to help you stay undetected and preserve your accounts.

### 1. Always Use Reasonable Settings
The biggest mistake most players make is pushing their settings to the absolute limit just because they can.
* **Keep sliders realistic:** Even if a client allows you to set your Reach to 6 blocks or your Autoclicker to 30 CPS, you should always stick to values that mimic human capabilities.
* **Randomization:** Using no randomization makes it incredibly easy for heuristic checks to flag you. 

### 2. You can still get staff banned
Just because an anticheat won't flag you doesn't mean your settings don't look obvious.
* **Staff Bans:** If you look blatant, you will eventually get staff banned. Always prioritize looking legit in replays.

### 3. Always Test on Alternate Accounts
Never use a potentially unsafe module on your main without prior testing on an alternate account.
* **Use Undetectable Modules:** Developers constantly push patches and detection updates to their anticheats without any public announcements. A setting that wasn't detected yesterday could get you banned today.
* **Use Alts:** Keep a few cheap alternate accounts specifically for testing and adjusting your settings before taking them to your main.

### 4. Ping
Your ping plays a massive role in configuring your client to give you the biggest advantage.
* **High Ping Advantage/Disadvantage:** If you have high latency, certain lag modules might actually give you a disadvantage.

---

:::tip

If you find yourself questioning whether a setting or module is too blatant, it probably is.
