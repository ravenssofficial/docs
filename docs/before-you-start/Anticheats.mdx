Most anticheats won't instantly ban you after your violation level reaches a certain amount.  
Instead, they will delay your ban by some time to make it harder to figure out what got you banned.

### Anticheat Classifications

While server-side anticheats have evolved, most modern ones fall into three primary categories based on how they process player data and handle violations:

#### 1. Active Anticheats
This is the traditional style of anticheat, commonly seen in older systems like **NCP (NoCheatPlus)**, **Vulcan**, and older iterations of **Watchdog**. Instead of silently tracking you, they immediately react to cheats in real-time.

* **How they work:** The server monitors incoming packets and rejects any actions that exceed standard limits, such as moving too fast or reaching too far. If a violation is caught, the server drops that packet and forces your client back to its last recorded legal coordinates.
* **The Flag:** This creates the infamous **rubberbanding** effect where you get dragged back and glitch in place. In combat, it results in silent hit-cancellation, meaning your hits simply won't register if you trigger a reach, hitbox or rotation check.

#### 2. Prediction-Based Anticheats
These are currently the most popular and effective solutions in the PvP scene, with **Watchdog** and **Polar** being the most known. 
* **How they work:** Instead of just checking if you are moving too fast, the server runs a parallel simulation of the Minecraft physics engine. It predicts exactly where your character should be on the next tick based on your inputs.
* **The Flag:** If your actual position, velocity, or movement packets deviate even slightly from the server's predicted path, you will trigger a flag.

#### 3. Silent Anticheats
Some servers prefer not to let cheaters know they've been caught immediately.
* **How they work:** Instead of dragging you back or cancelling your hits the moment you flag, these checks run completely silently in the background.
* **The Bans:** Every violation increases your internal violation level (VL). Once it crosses a certain threshold, your account is banned.

---

:::warning[Mitigations]

Modern anticheats don't always ban you instantly. Instead, many top-tier servers now use **mitigations** to silently ruin your gameplay after a flag. 
If you trigger certain checks, the anticheat might reduce your damage output so your hits deal barely any damage.  
If you suddenly feel like you can't trade hits or do damage, you are likely being mitigated by the server.

:::
