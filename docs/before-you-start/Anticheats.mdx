# Anticheats

:::warning[Delayed Bans]

Most anticheats won't instantly ban you after your violation level reaches a certain threshold. Instead, they delay your ban by minutes or hours to prevent you from pinpointing the exact setting or module that triggered the flag.

:::

---

## Check Types

Anticheats process player data using two fundamental types of checks:

### Direct Checks (Hard Checks)

Direct checks monitor actions that are physically impossible in vanilla Minecraft under any network conditions.

* **How they work:** These checks continuously measure strict limits like maximum reach distance and knockback. Because these events cannot occur naturally, direct checks are very reliable and result in instant hit cancellation, rubberbanding, or bans.

### Heuristic Checks (Soft Checks)

Heuristic checks analyze patterns, trends, and behavioral anomalies over time rather than evaluating single events.

* **How they work:** These checks evaluate randomization for various actions and network behavior (such as convenient ping spikes with BackTrack). These checks help anticheats distinguish between convenient lag and cheats.

:::note

Hypixel's Watchdog rarely relies on heuristic checks, unlike top tier anticheats such as Polar.

:::

## Anticheat Classifications

Most modern systems fall into three primary categories based on how they process player data and handle violations:

### 1. Active Anticheats
This is the traditional style of anticheat, commonly seen in systems like **NCP (NoCheatPlus)**, **Vulcan**, and older versions of **Watchdog**. Instead of silently tracking you, they immediately react to violations in real-time.

* **How they work:** The server monitors incoming packets and rejects any actions that exceed standard limits, such as moving too fast or reaching too far. If a violation is caught, the server drops that packet and forces your client back to its last recorded legal coordinates.

### 2. Prediction-Based Anticheats
These are currently the most effective solutions in the PvP scene, with **Watchdog** and **Polar** being the most prominent examples.

* **How they work:** Instead of just checking whether you are moving too fast, the server runs a parallel simulation of the Minecraft physics engine. It predicts exactly where your character should be on the next tick based on your inputs.
If your actual position, velocity, or movement packets deviate even slightly from the server's predicted path, you trigger a flag and the server corrects your movement.

### 3. Silent Anticheats
Some servers prefer not to let cheaters know they have been caught immediately.

* **How they work:** Instead of dragging you back or cancelling your hits the moment you flag, these checks run completely silently in the background. Every violation increases your internal violation level (VL). Once it crosses a set threshold, your account is queued for a delayed ban.

---

:::warning[Mitigations]

Modern anticheats don't always ban you. Instead, many top-tier servers use **mitigations** to silently degrade your gameplay after a flag. If you trigger certain checks, the anticheat might reduce your damage output or alter your knockback taken. If you suddenly feel like you can't trade hits or deal damage, you are likely being mitigated by the server.

:::
