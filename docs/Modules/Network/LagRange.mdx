## Overview

Lag Range uses network latency manipulation to delay sending your position packets to the server mid-combat. From their perspective, you appear to be slightly further back or lagged behind, preventing them from landing hits on you. Meanwhile, you can easily secure first hits and maintain spacing.

While similar to Backtrack, Lag Range is significantly more difficult to detect as it closely mimics legitimate ping spikes during combat.

---

:::danger[Hypixel Mitigation Patch]

Just like with other packet-choking modules, Hypixel has fully mitigated this behavior. The server forces delayed movement packets to process in short, metered bursts instead of executing them all at once. This forces your client to catch up smoothly to your actual coordinates, completely eliminating any spacing or reach advantage.

:::
