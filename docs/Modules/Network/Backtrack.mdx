## Overview

Backtrack works by delaying incoming entity packets from the server, causing other players to appear completely frozen or lagged in place just as they are about to move out of your reach. Because you hit their "delayed" hitboxes, you can chain additional hits that would normally be impossible.

Since this module is fully lag-based and technically replicates a laggy player, it does not violate vanilla Minecraft reach limits on paper, making it a safer alternative for servers where regular reach is patched.

---

:::warning[Heuristical Detections]

Although it was rarely detected before 2024, most top tier anticheats can detect this module easily on high settings.
However, it is still considered **significantly safer** than traditional Reach modules, as it only mimics lag.

:::

---

:::danger[Manual Backtrack]

**Manual (i.e. reach-based) Backtrack** does not modify your actual connection packets, which causes modern anticheats to detect your attacks as traditional Reach or Hitbox modifications. always use **Lag-Based Backtrack** instead.

:::
