## Overview

Blink intercepts and holds back all movement packets sent from your client to the server. While active, other players see your character frozen in place, allowing you to safely move to a different position. When deactivated, the module releases all accumulated packets at once, causing you to instantly "blink" or teleport to your current coordinates from the perspective of everyone else.

---

:::warning[Staff Monitoring & Detection]

While Blink leverages simulated network latency, it can occasionally trigger flags and kicks on modern anticheats if packets are held for too long. Furthermore, server staff can easily detect and manually ban players who abuse this module. Using it too frequently during critical moments makes it obvious that you aren't just experiencing convenient network lag.

:::

---

:::danger[Hypixel Mitigation Patch]

Just like with other packet-choking modules, Hypixel has fully mitigated this behavior. The server forces delayed movement packets to process in short, metered bursts instead of executing them all at once. This forces your client to catch up smoothly to your actual coordinates, completely eliminating any spacing or reach advantage.

:::
