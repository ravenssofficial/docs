## Overview

FakeLag artificially delays communication with the server. Unlike modules that choke packets erratically, your movement remains smooth rather than stuttery, making your position unpredictable during PvP.

The module is completely undetectable. It simulates genuine latency instead of spoofing your ping or sending invalid packets, meaning the server treats you as a standard high ping user.

---

:::note[Server Kicks & Hit Registration]

If your configured latency is too high or held for too long, the server may fail to register your hits, trigger setbacks, or completely disconnect you due to packet loss thresholds.

:::
