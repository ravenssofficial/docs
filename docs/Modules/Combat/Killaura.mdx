## Overview

Killaura automates combat by managing both your aiming and clicking inputs simultaneously. On premium clients, this module functions essentially as a combined Autoclicker and AimAssist, making it significantly safer when configured properly. 

However, it still carries a risk of staff bans if your rotations look too fast.

---

## Configuration & Mechanics

* **Rotation Speed:** Set a speed that feels responsive but avoids instant, robotic snaps. The camera movement needs to look organic to bypass heuristic checks.
* **Maximum Angle:** Limit your attack angle to a maximum of 120° to prevent the module from accidentally snapping to players behind you.
* **Client Choice:** Avoid using Killaura on free clients. Free clients sometimes rely on outdated exploits that can trigger bans, whereas premium clients focus on simulating legitimate human behavior.

---

:::tip[Verification]

If you are unsure whether your rotation settings look natural, play a match on a server that supports replays and review your perspective from the replay system.

:::
