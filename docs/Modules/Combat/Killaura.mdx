:::danger[Movement Fixation Required]

You must enable proper movement fixation in your client's settings. Otherwise, you will be banned on every relevant anticheat.

:::

### Detection and Configuration

This module is highly susceptible to flags and bans depending on your settings and the client you are using.

*   **Max Angle:** To stop the module from attacking enemies behind you, do not go above 120°
*   **Rotation speed:** While you should use a speed that makes the killaura feel snappy, make sure that it won't lock onto enemies instantly and looks legit.
*   **Free Clients:** Avoid using this module on free clients. Their Killauras are made with blatant cheating and using exploits to bypass anticheats in mind, instead of reproducing legitimate behaviour.
