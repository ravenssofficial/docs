## Overview

Reach expands the physical bounds of your combat interactions, allowing you to hit entities from further away than normally possible, thus making it very susceptible to detections.

---

## Detection Mechanics

* **Hitbox Calculations:** Anticheats calculate the exact distance between your character's hitbox and the target's hitbox at the precise millisecond your hit packet arrives.
* **Lag Compensation:** While high ping can occasionally cause legitimate hits to appear extended on your screen, server-side anticheats actively compensate for latency to filter out false positives and catch artificial reach instantly.

---

:::tip[A Safer Alternative]

If you want a safe way to maximize your combat distance, enable **Multipoint** in your AimAssist settings. This feature targets the closest edge of your opponent's hitbox, helping you consistently land perfect 3.0-block vanilla hits without breaking game limits.

:::
