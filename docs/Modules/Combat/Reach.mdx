---

:::danger[Extremely Detectable]

This module is detected on almost every anticheat at virtually any value.  
Modern anticheats continuously track player coordinates and ping, making even small reach increments extremely easy to flag.  
Avoid using this module completely.

:::

### How Anticheats Catch Reach

Unlike other modules that mimic human input, reach directly breaks the physical limits of the game, making it a primary target for server-side checks.

*   **Advanced Calculations:** Anticheats calculate the exact distance between your hitbox and the enemy's hitbox at the millisecond your hit registers.
*   **Lag Compensation:** While higher ping can sometimes make legitimate hits look long, anticheats account for latency. 

---

:::tip

If you want a safer way to extend your hit distance, enable multipoint in AimAssist settings.  
It aims to the closest point on your enemy's hitbox, letting you get perfect 3.0 block hits more often

:::
