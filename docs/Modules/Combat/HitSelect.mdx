### Mechanics and Usage

This module is **completely undetectable** because it only filters when your client registers hits, which mimics perfect human timing.

*   **Attack Timing** Instead of spamming, it will time your hits so you can keep combos while losing speed.
*   **Knockback Reduction:** Because it makes you attack in short bursts, it will be completely 
*   **Completely Undetectable:** Since it only controls the timing of your manual clicks, it can't be detected at all, but still offers a massive advantages.

---

:::tip

You can freely use external autoclickers or just manually click yourself while this module is active—HitSelect will still do its job and filter your inputs perfectly.

:::
