## Overview

Velocity alters how your character responds to incoming hit packets. By lowering or stopping vertical and horizontal knockback, it allows you to maintain your positioning and push through enemy trades easily. 

Safety depends entirely on the method you use. Directly changing movement data will trigger instant server-side flags, while simulating player mechanics provides much better concealment.

---

## Modes & Detection

Most clients offer several ways to handle knockback reduction, each carrying a different level of risk:

### Vanilla / Normal

This mode directly modifies your packets to reduce the knockback you take.

:::danger[Highly Detectable]

This is highly detectable on modern anticheats at almost any percentage. Avoid using it on servers with an anticheat.

:::

### Lag

This method uses network manipulation to delay your incoming knockback packets. It effectively reduces your knockback, especially while you are stuck in an enemy combo. Like any other lag-based method, this remains risky against top-tier anticheats.

:::info[Standalone Module]

On certain clients, this specific functionality is broken out into a standalone module called **KnockbackDelay**.

:::

### JumpReset

Automatically triggers a jump on the exact tick you take damage to naturally absorb your knockback. To stay safe on strict anticheats, keep your chance between 20%-35%. If the client you're using features an accuracy slider for the jumps, lower it slightly to remain completely undetected.

:::info[Standalone Module]

On certain clients, this specific functionality is broken out into a standalone module called **JumpReset**.

:::

---

:::tip[Safety Recommendation]

If your main goal is to look completely legitimate and avoid flags while maintaining a massive advantage, stick exclusively to the JumpReset mode.

:::
