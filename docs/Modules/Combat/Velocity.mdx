  Reduces or completely cancels the knockback you take from attacks and projectiles.
</div>

---

### Modes and Detection

This module has multiple modes on most clients, each carrying a different level of risk:

#### Vanilla / Normal
:::danger[Highly Detectable]

This mode directly modifies your motion packets. It is highly detectable on anticheats at any value. Avoid using it.

:::

#### Lag
Uses network lag to delay your knockback packets, making you take less knockback, especially while you are being comboed.
As with any other lag-based method, this is risky on top-tier anticheats

:::info[Standalone Module]

On certain clients, this feature will be a standalone module called **KnockbackDelay**.

:::

#### JumpReset
Automatically jumps the exact tick you take damage to redirect and reduce your knockback.  
As long as you keep your chance between 20-35%, you should be fine.  
If your client allows you to lower the accuracy of the jumps, do that as well.

:::info[Standalone Module]

On certain clients, this feature will be a standalone module called **JumpReset**.

:::

---

:::tip

If you want to look completely legitimate, and avoid bans, stick to JumpReset.

:::
