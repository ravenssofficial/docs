### Combat Modes & Mechanics

Most clients offer a few different ways to customize how the module behaves:

*   **Automatic / Prediction:** This mode automatically blockhits immediately after you land a hit, or predicts your enemy's next hit to time the block perfectly. It looks very natural and is completely undetectable.
*   **Manual:** This mode spams the block button only while you are actively holding it down. Just like the automatic mode, this is entirely safe and undetectable.

---

:::warning[Keep in mind]

Certain **lag modes** and bypass options built into Blockhit are easily flagged. Modern anticheats like Polar and Watchdog have fully patched these methods, so using them will get you banned. Stick to the standard blocking modes.

:::
