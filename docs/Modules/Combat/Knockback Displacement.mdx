## Overview

Knockback Displacement automates a high-level PvP technique where looking away immediately during a sprint-hit alters the direction of your enemy's knockback. 

While the module mimics real mechanical movements, aggressive settings can look incredibly obvious to spectators and staff. Safety depends heavily on keeping your rotation angles and timing realistic.

---

## Mechanics & Configuration

* **Displacement Angles:** Avoid going over **90°** to make the flicks look believable.
* **Flick Delay:** Keep your movements looking organic by using a higher delay between flicks (e.g., ~450ms).
* **Staff Bans:** Using small delays or extreme angles will make your character flick erratically, making you an easy target for manual staff bans.

---

:::tip[Hide Your Rotations]

If you want to hide the flicks shakes from other players and staff, enable the **Hide Rotations** option if your client supports it.  
This setting chokes your outgoing packets right before the flick occurs and releases them only after your camera rotates back.

:::
