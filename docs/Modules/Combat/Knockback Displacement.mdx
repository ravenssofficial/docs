### Mechanics and Configuration

This module automates the high-level PvP technique where looking away during a sprint-hit alters the direction your enemy will take knockback.  
While it mimics real human mechanics, aggressive settings will look highly unnatural.

*   **Flick Delay:** To keep your movements looking organic, make sure to use a higher delay (e.g. 450ms)
*   **Angles:** Keep the displacement angles realistic, don't go over 90°.
*   **Staff Bans:** With certain settings, this module can look incredibly obvious, leading to a staff ban.

---

:::tip[Hide Your Rotations]

If you don't want other players or staff to see your character flicking, enable this option in the module's settings.   
It chokes your packets right before flicking and unchokes them as the module rotates you back.

:::
