## Overview

Autoclicker automates your left-click inputs to deliver consistent attack speeds during PvP. Since it operates by sending standard client-side click packets, it is structurally safe from direct server-side packet bans. However, detection entirely depends on your configured CPS (Clicks Per Second) limits and how well your client randomizes the delays between clicks.

---

## Mechanics & Configuration

* **CPS Customization:** The maximum safe CPS you can run is determined by server-side anticheat checks. Strict heuristic engines analyze click consistency to flag unnatural patterns.
* **Randomization Ranges:** On strict servers, always maintain a gap of at least **4 CPS** between your minimum and maximum limits (e.g., 10–14 CPS) to break up consistency.
* **Static CPS Profiles:** If your client only supports a fixed CPS value, enable every available randomization to prevent detections.
* **Hypixel Watchdog Leniency:** Hypixel is a notable exception regarding click heuristics; its anticheat does not actively analyze click-delay randomization, allowing you to safely use static configurations up to 20 CPS.

---

:::tip[Double Click Simulation]

If your client features a **Butterfly Click** or **Simulate Double Clicks** option, enable it. This setting is crucial for bypassing advanced heuristic checks at speeds above 15 CPS, as it mimics the physical double-registration of mechanical mouse switches. If your client lacks double-click simulation, limit your speed to a maximum of 14 CPS.

:::
