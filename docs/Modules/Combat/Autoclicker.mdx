## Overview

Autoclicker automates your left click inputs to deliver consistent CPS. Since it operates by clicking for you, it is structurally safe from direct checks. However, detection entirely depends on your configured CPS (Clicks Per Second) limits and how well your client randomizes the delays between clicks.

---

## Mechanics & Configuration

* **CPS Customization:** The maximum safe CPS you can run is determined by server-side anticheat checks. Some servers don't even check for consistency below a certain value.
* **Randomization Ranges:** On strict servers, always maintain a gap of at least **4 CPS** between your minimum and maximum limits (e.g., 10–14 CPS) to break up consistency.
* **Fixed CPS:** If your client only supports a fixed CPS value, enable every available randomization to prevent detections.

:::note[Hypixel]

Hypixel is a notable exception regarding CPS checks, its' anticheat does not actively analyze click-delay randomization, allowing you to safely use no randimzation up to 20 CPS, although we recommend using ~15 CPS for best hit registration.

:::
---

:::tip[Double Click Simulation]

If your client features a **Butterfly Click** or **Simulate Double Clicks** option, enable it. This setting is crucial for bypassing advanced checks at speeds above 15 CPS, as it mimics the physical double-registration of mechanical mouse switches. If your client lacks double-click simulation, limit your speed to a maximum of 15 CPS on strict anticheats.

:::
