### Detection & CPS Limits

How high you can push your CPS heavily depends on the **anticheat leniency** of the server you are playing on. Some older or more relaxed anticheats will let you click as fast as you want, while modern ones have strict heuristic checks that analyze your clicking patterns.

*   **Recommended settings:** To stay safe on strict servers, always keep a gap of at least **4 CPS** between your minimum and maximum limits (e.g., 10-14 CPS).
*   **Fixated CPS:** If your client does not let you pick a CPS range, you should enable every available randomization setting, especially if the anticheat you're on has stricter autoclicker checks.
*   **Anticheat Leniency:** Most modern anticheats have strict autoclicker checks. A major exception is Hypixel's Watchdog, which doesn't check for click randomization at all, meaning you can safely use any value under 20 CPS without getting flagged.

---

:::tip 

If your client has a **Butterfly** or **Simulate Double Clicks** mode, turn it on. This is essential if you want to push for 16+ CPS on strict servers because it mimics how real mouse switches double-register when double clicking. If your client lacks these simulation settings, do not go over 15 CPS.

:::
