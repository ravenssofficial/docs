## Overview

AimAssist provides subtle camera adjustments to keep your crosshair locked onto opponents during combat. Unlike blatant combat modules, AimAssist is designed to work alongside your manual mouse movements, making your tracking look completely legit. 

Because the module only simulates mouse movements on your screen and does not bypass any vanilla limitations, it is completely undetectable by anticheats if the client you're using has good randomization.

---

## Mechanics

* **Sensitivity Scaling:** On some clients, the module scales directly with your in-game sensitivity to calculate the actual aiming speed.
* **Targeting Angles:** If the client you're using supports it, restricting the minimal aiming angle to around ~15° prevents detections on very advanced anticheats.

---

:::warning[Safety]

Make sure to use the module to help you aim, rather than aim for you.  
Using it as an AimBot can be obvious to staff and sometimes even anticheats.

:::
