## Overview

AimAssist provides subtle camera adjustments to keep your crosshair locked onto opponents during combat. Unlike blatant combat modules, AimAssist is designed to work alongside your manual mouse movements, making your tracking look completely legit. 

Because the module only generates simulated mouse movements on your screen and does not bypass any vanilla limitations or send invalid rotation packets to the server, it is completely undetectable by server-side anticheat engines.

---

## Mechanics

* **Sensitivity Scaling:** On most clients, the module scales directly with your in-game sensitivity to calculate the actual aiming speed.
* **Targeting Angles:** If your client supports it, restricting the minimal aiming angle to around ~15° prevents detections on very advanced anticheats.

---

:::warning[Safety]

Make sure to use the module to help you aim, rather than aim for you.  
Using it as an AimBot will be obvious to staff and sometimes even anticheats.

:::
