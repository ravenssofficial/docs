### Detection & Mechanics

This module is completely undetectable as long as you play with reasonable settings. 

*   **Sensitivity Scaling:** On some clients, AimAssist scales directly with your in-game sensitivity to calculate the speed.
*   **Anticheat Checks:** Modern anticheats flag players who constantly lock onto the exact center of a hitbox or the closest point perfectly.
*   **How to stay safe:** If your client has the option, set the minimal angle for aiming to ~15°.

---

:::warning

AimAssist is designed to assist your natural aiming, not play the game for you.  
Using overly aggressive settings and not being careful can still lead to staff bans.

:::
