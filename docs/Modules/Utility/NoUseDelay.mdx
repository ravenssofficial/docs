## Overview

When playing on multiplayer servers, high latency causes a noticeable delay when consuming food, drinking potions, or drawing a bow. Your client normally waits for a server confirmation packet before it registers that the action is finished, making item use feel slower.

NoUseDelay bypasses this lag by releasing your right-click input slightly earlier than vanilla Minecraft would. By predicting the completion of the action based on your latency, it makes consuming items on high ping feel as fast and responsive as if you're playing on singleplayer.

---

:::note[Latency Fluctuations]

Because this module relies on predicting your network connection, sudden lag spikes or ping fluctuations might cause your client to release the right-click too early. If this happens, the server will reject the action, and the item will not actually be consumed or used.

:::
