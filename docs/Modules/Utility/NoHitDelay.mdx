## Overview

In Minecraft 1.8, missing a swing triggers a short mechanical delay before your next attack can register.  
NoHitDelay removes this penalty entirely, ensuring that your clicks register instantly even if you just missed a swing.

---

:::warning[Anticheat Detection]

Because modern anticheats can monitor air-swing packets, running this module completely raw on competitive servers carries a risk of detection, though incredibly low.  

If the client you are using has a **Legit** mode option and you're playing on an exceptionally strict anticheat, you should enable it to stay undetected.

:::
