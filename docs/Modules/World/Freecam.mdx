## Overview

Freecam allows you to freely move your camera around the map while your physical character remains stationary. It is perfect for scouting out enemy positions, checking behind walls, or looking for hidden bases without exposing yourself to danger.

Since the module works entirely client-side, simply letting you fly your camera around without sending abnormal movement packets for your actual body, it's completely undetectable by server-side anticheats under normal usage.

---

:::danger[Allow Interacting]

Don't enable the **Allow Interacting** option within your Freecam settings. Modern anticheats will flag this instantly as a severe reach or illegal interaction hack, resulting in a ban.

:::
