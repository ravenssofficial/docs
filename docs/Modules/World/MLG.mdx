## Overview

MLG automates the clutching process by detecting when you are falling and instantly placing a water bucket or cobweb right underneath your feet a split second before you land to completely negate fall damage.

---

:::tip[100% Undetectable]

This module is **100% undetectable** by server-side anticheats. Because it simply automates a normal right-click interaction at the perfect block coordinate right before impact. It's something a highly skilled player could do legitimately, so anticheats cannot distinguish it from a perfect manual clutch.

:::
