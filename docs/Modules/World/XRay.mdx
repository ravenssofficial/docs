## Overview

XRay modifies the client-side rendering of the world, making stone, dirt, and other filler blocks completely transparent while keeping valuable resources visible. This allows you to pinpoint the exact location of rich ore veins without wasting time strip-mining blindly.

From a technical standpoint, this module is 100% undetectable by server-side anticheats because it operates entirely on your screen, without sending abnormal packets or illegal data to the server.

---

:::warning[Staff Monitoring & Moderation]

Even though anticheats cannot detect XRay, server staff and moderators can. Digging in perfect straight lines from one diamond vein to another, making unnatural turns or immediately finding hidden bases will look incredibly obvious to staff.

:::
