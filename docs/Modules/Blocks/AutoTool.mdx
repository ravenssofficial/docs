### Overview

AutoTool is a simple but extremely useful utility module that saves you from manually scrolling through your hotbar when mining or gathering resources. The moment you start digging, the client instantly swaps to the correct item (shovel, pickaxe, axe, or shears).

This module is undetectable by all means on any anticheat and any server, as it only selects items for you.

---

:::tip

To stop AutoTool from switching to tools mid-combat, turn on the "only while sneaking" option in the module's settings.

:::
