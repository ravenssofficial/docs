### Overview

Clutch is a utility module that automatically block-clutches for you. The moment you are hit off a bridge or fall from a height, the client instantly selects blocks and places them to catch you.

While premium clients mimic human movement and are generally safe, free or badly coded clients can flag anticheats or look way too blatant, causing staff bans.

---

:::tip[Damaged Conditional]

Sometimes, the client might start clutching for you randomly when you don't actually need it (like jumping while bridging).  
To fix this, set the activation condition to **Damaged** or simply bind the module to a key and toggle it on in combat.

:::
