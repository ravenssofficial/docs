## Overview

Bridge Assist is a utility module designed to help you speed/ninja bridge. It monitors your position and automatically presses the sneak key right as you approach the edge of a block, preventing you from falling off.

This module is generally undetectable on most standard anticheats since it simply automates a normal movement keypress.

---

## Anticheat Safety

While generally safe, highly advanced anticheats like **Polar** analyze the exact delay of your sneaks. 
If your client sneaks with a static, non-randomized delay every single time, these anticheats will easily flag and ban you.

Most premium, paid clients feature built-in sneak delay randomization to mimic human timing and bypass these checks, whereas lower-quality free clients often lack this safeguard.

---

:::tip[Accidental sneaking]

To stop accidental sneaking at the end of blocks, enable the **Only While Sneaking" option in the settings.

:::
