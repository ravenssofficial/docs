## Overview

Refill automates the process of dragging replacements (such as health potions and soup) from your main inventory directly into your active hotbar slots.

To keep your account completely safe from bans, you should always configure the module to use realistic delays.

---

:::tip

This module is incredibly useful on modes like NoDebuff and SoupPVP

:::
