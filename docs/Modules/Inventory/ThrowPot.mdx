## Overview

ThrowPot automates the process of selecting and throwing health or buff potions from your hotbar, allowing you to heal instantly mid-fight without manually swapping slots. 

While you should still configure realistic delays to keep your actions looking natural, anticheats are generally much more lenient when checking potion-throwing speeds compared to other inventory modules.

---

:::tip[NoDebuff Mode]

This module is incredibly useful for fast-paced gamemodes like **NoDebuff**, where maintaining your health bar under pressure is key to winning fights.

:::
