## Overview

Sprint automates the vanilla sprinting mechanic, eliminating the need to double-tap your forward key or hold down a designated sprint button. As long as you are moving forward, the module will keep your sprint active.

Under normal settings, this is completely safe and fully compliant with server-side physics limits.

---

:::danger[Avoid Impossible Sprint Conditionals]

Do not enable custom options or conditionals that force you to sprint in situations where vanilla physics would make it impossible.  
Forcing a sprint while blindly walking backward, holding sneak, or being in an inventory will flag modern anticheats and result in a swift ban.

:::
