## Overview

In Minecraft PvP, your very first hit while sprinting deals significantly more knockback. SprintReset automates this mechanic, allowing you to easily trap opponents in combos without having to manually reset your sprint.

This module usually has 2 modes:

### Tapping Modes (W-Tap, S-Tap, Shift-Tap)  
These modes mimic legitimate keyboard inputs by briefly stopping your forward momentum or sneaking for a split second after a hit to reset your sprint.

### NoStop
This mode instantly resets your sprint without stopping your character. Even though this might sound blatant, it is completely safe and works on almost every major anticheat.

---

:::warning[Toggle-Sprint Compatibility]

To ensure SprintReset functions correctly, you must not use external toggle-sprint mods.  
Instead, make sure to pair it with the built-in [Sprint](/realravenss-docs/Modules/Movement/Sprint) module from your client.

:::

---

:::info[Alternative Names]

If you are a Vape V4 user, this feature is not a standalone module. Instead, you can find it as the sprint reset option inside the [KeepSprint](/realravenss-docs/Modules/Movement/KeepSprint) module.  

For maximum safety, make sure to set the speed multiplier to 0.6x.

:::
