## Overview

In vanilla Minecraft, there is a built-in cooldown that prevents you from jumping immediately after landing.  
NoJumpDelay bypasses this restriction, allowing you to jump again the exact millisecond you touch the ground.

Since this only automates the jump timing on your client and keeps your movement within normal physics limits, it is 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::tip

This module is perfect for maintaining maximum speed while navigating tight parkour paths, as you will never lose speed to vanilla jump exhaustion.

:::
