## Overview

Freelook allows you to rotate your camera freely around your character without changing the direction you are walking or looking.  
It is extremely useful for checking your surroundings or seeing if someone is chasing you.

This module only changes how the camera is rendered on your screen, making it 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::tip[Look Behind Safely]

You can use this while bridging or during chases to check behind you without ruining your movement or slowing down.

:::
