### Overview

Indicators displays visual warning arrows on your screen when a projectile is flying toward you.  
It helps you track incoming threats like arrows, ender pearls, splash potions, and fireballs so you can easily dodge them.

Since this module only visualizes entity data already sent to your client by the server, it is 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::tip[Dodge Threats Instantly]

This is highly effective in bow fights or BedWars matches where players spam fireballs. The indicators let you react to projectiles coming from outside your field of view without having to look directly at them.

:::
