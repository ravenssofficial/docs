## Overview

ESP (Extrasensory Perception) is a core render module that highlights other players, or mobs through solid terrain, allowing you to easily track their movement, distance, and health.

Since this is a purely client-side visual module, it is 100% undetectable on any server or anticheat.

---

### Common Modes

* **Box:** Draws a 2D or 3D box around targets. Very lightweight and FPS-friendly.
* **Skeleton:** Renders the actual bone structure of players to show their exact posture and orientation.
* **Outline:** Highlights the entire character model with a glowing edge.

---

:::tip[FPS Drops]

If you are playing on a low-end PC, stick to the **Box** or **2D** mode. Outline shaders and skeleton renders can sometimes drop your frames in lobby areas with tons of players.

:::
