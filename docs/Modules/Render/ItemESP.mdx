### Overview

ItemESP highlights all dropped items through blocks and at a distance. It makes it incredibly easy to find loot after a fight or locate resources that fell into dark areas.

Since this module only visualizes item entities that your client already knows about, it is 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::tip[Find Dropped Loot Instantly]

This is perfect for sky battles or fast-paced PvP modes where loot from dead players gets scattered everywhere. It saves you from missing valuable items like golden apples, pearls, or stacked gear hidden in blocks.

:::
