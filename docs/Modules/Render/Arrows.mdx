## Overview

Arrows is a clean, minimal render module designed to improve your situational awareness. It draws 2D arrows around your crosshair pointing in the direction of nearby players who are outside your field of view (FOV). 

Since this module only visualizes player position data that your client already has, it is 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::tip[Perfect for Fast-Paced PvP]

This module is incredibly useful in gamemodes like **BedWars** and **SkyWars**, where players constantly try to sneak up or jump down on you from behind. 

:::
