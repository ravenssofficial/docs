### Overview

Tracers points a line from your screen directly to every player in your vicinity. This allows you to immediately see their exact direction and position, regardless of walls or their visibility on your screen.

Since this module only visualizes the positional data already provided to your client, it is 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::tip[Prevent Sneak Attacks]

This tool is highly effective in team modes or massive maps where players can easily sneak up from behind or hide in nearby structures. You will always see a line pointing to their location before they can reach you.

:::
