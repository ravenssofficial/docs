## Overview

Anti Debuff is a render-focused utility that cleans up your screen by removing negative visual effects like Blindness, Nausea and FOV alteration with Slowness. 

Since the visual cleanup happens purely on your client, this part of the module is 100% safe and undetectable.

---

:::danger[Remove Effects Option]

Many clients feature a setting often called **Remove Effects** (or functional anti-debuff) which attempts to actually clear the server-side potion effects from your character.

* This is **extremely blatant and easily flagged** by almost every modern anticheat. 
* Keep this specific option **disabled** on any server with an anticheat system.

:::
